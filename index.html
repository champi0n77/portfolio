Welcome to my portfolio

Hello, I’m Victor Agbo

I am a Computer Science student at MIVA Open University, and this site is both my personal portfolio and my COS 106 term project. Here you can read about my background, browse the things I have built, plan coursework with my academic planner, and get in touch.

I build for the web and enjoy the systems underneath it: I like taking an idea from a rough sketch to a working interface, then working out the logic and data behind it. I care about clear, responsive design that still behaves on a small phone, and about writing code I can explain line by line.

Biography

A little about me

I am Victor Agbo, an undergraduate student of Computer Science at MIVA Open University. My interest in technology started with curiosity about how the pages I used every day were actually put together, and it turned into a habit of building things myself.

Since then I have worked on web applications and on smaller system-level exercises, because I want to understand what happens below the frameworks — how data is stored, how a server responds, and why some interfaces feel effortless while others do not. I am currently deepening my front-end skills with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript while studying the fundamentals of computing.
